An Austin sprinkler visit is four jobs. Name a broken head vs a line leak vs a zone valve vs a controller before you sign the ticket.

Homeowners mix a $head swap with an underground leak hunt, a valve rebuild, or a new clock.

Ask for the TCEQ irrigator license and whether the clock will be reset to current Austin-area watering rules.

  • Ask whether this visit is a broken head or riser, an underground lateral or mainline leak, a zone valve or solenoid, or a controller and wiring problem.
  • Ask how they will find a buried leak or lost valve before they open the yard.
  • Ask for the TCEQ licensed irrigator number that will be on the work.
  • Ask whether a head swap is being bundled with a zone split or redesign — write those as separate lines.
  • Ask whether the repair includes resetting the clock to current local watering days.
  • Call a shop that publishes a phone on its own website.
