Common questions

Is a broken sprinkler head the same job as an underground leak?

No. A broken head or riser is usually at the surface. A lateral or mainline leak is underground and takes locating. A zone that will not shut off is often a valve. A zone that will not start can be a valve, wire, or controller. Ask which of those is quoted.

Who licenses irrigators in Texas?

The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality licenses landscape irrigators. Ask for the license number. This site is not TCEQ and is not the City of Austin.

Why is a leak quote higher than a head swap?

Finding and opening a buried line is different work from swapping a head. A controller or valve rebuild is another job. Ask for the diagnosis first. This site does not set prices.

Do I need a new system if one zone is brown?

Ask whether the brown patch is a clogged or misaligned head, low pressure from a leak, a stuck valve, or a zone that is too large. A full redesign is a separate decision.

Will the repair reset my watering days?

Austin-area watering restrictions change. Ask whether the clock will be set to current local rules after the repair. Confirm that with the City of Austin or your water utility — not this site.
